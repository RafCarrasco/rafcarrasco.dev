Official **MET — Michigan English Test** certificate, attesting **B2 (Upper-Intermediate)** level, issued by Michigan Language Assessment in partnership with Cambridge Assessment English and University of Michigan.

Listening and reading assessment taken in April 2019. Registration nº 999042016.

The MET is one of the most internationally recognized English proficiency exams, equivalent to Cambridge FCE and TOEFL ITP at the CEFR B2 level.
